# VibeSync Ethical Advertising Policy

Copyright © 2026 VibeSync. All rights reserved.

VibeSync advertising follows a user-first model: ads inform and monetize without coercing the user experience.

## Placement
- Swipe deck: 1 ad after every 5 profile swipes.
- Story feed: 1 sponsored story after every 5 user stories viewed.
- Every ad is clearly marked sponsored and has an immediate skip/dismiss action.

## Privacy
No device identifier is sold or shared with third-party ad brokers for behavioral profiling. Contextual targeting should use minimal local/approved campaign attributes.

## Pricing
Current product specification defines CPM tiers including Standard Feed Deck ₹150/1,000, Standard Story Ad ₹150/1,000, HD Video Interstitial ₹200/1,000, Prime Blitz ₹250/1,000, plus a hyperlocal surcharge. fileciteturn146file0L31-L43

## Prohibited patterns
- unskippable countdowns
- deceptive close controls
- hidden hitboxes
- dark-pattern navigation blocking
- ads that pretend to be ordinary user profiles without disclosure.
