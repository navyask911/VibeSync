## Current product safety position
The dating/romantic discovery product is designed around an 18+ default discovery boundary. Accounts under 18 must never be exposed to adult dating/discovery pools. The existing product specification also describes a separately isolated, guardian-consented 13–17 circle with contact-only communication; any production activation of that mode requires legal, trust-and-safety, child-protection, and store-policy review before launch. fileciteturn146file2L8-L17

## Engineering controls
- adults cannot discover, swipe on, or match with minors.
- age claims are not trusted from editable UI fields alone.
- under-18 profile visibility is explicitly isolated.
- block/report is available.
- safety escalation and preservation controls exist.
- sexual or romantic interactions involving minors are prohibited.

## Launch gate
Until age assurance and policy review are complete, production dating discovery should remain 18+ only.
