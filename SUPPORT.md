# VibeSync Support & Safety Requests

Copyright © 2026 VibeSync. All rights reserved.

Use the official support/reporting channel presented by the production VibeSync service for support requests.

## What support can handle

- account recovery and sign-in problems;
- privacy and personal-data requests;
- account-deletion requests;
- abuse, harassment and impersonation reports;
- business-account and wallet questions;
- security vulnerability reports;
- requests to review a moderation decision.

## What not to send

Never send passwords, OTP codes, private keys, recovery secrets, payment-card credentials, or a raw device address book to support.

For security reports, describe the affected feature, reproduction steps and impact without including live credentials or private user data.

## Privacy requests

A privacy/deletion request should contain only the information needed to identify and process the request. VibeSync may request reasonable verification before disclosing or deleting account information.

## Safety reports

Include enough information to identify the reported account/content, explain the safety issue and provide supporting context where safe to do so.

VibeSync may preserve relevant records while investigating safety or legal matters.

## Lawful requests

Authorized legal or government requests should use the official legal/request channel published with the production service. Requests should include the legal authority, scope, requested records and appropriate preservation information.

VibeSync cannot provide encrypted message plaintext that it does not possess.

## Response expectations

Support and safety requests are prioritized according to severity, legal requirements and available verification information. Public launch documentation should publish the current response process and any legally required grievance details.

**Do not rely on this document as a statutory notice by itself; legal review is required for the actual production grievance mechanism.**
